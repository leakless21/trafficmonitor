## 1 Evaluation Methodology

### 1.1 Datasets & Splits

| Task                         | Dataset                                                                                 | Split Strategy                                                                       | Samples                     |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Vehicle Detection & Counting | _Traffic-Flow-Test1_                                                                    | 70 % train – 15 % val – 15 % **test**, split by **camera** to avoid temporal leakage | 11 957 frames (test: 1 794) |
| Plate Detection              | _License-Plate-Recognition-5_                                                           | Same ratio, split by video                                                           | 19 024 images (test: 2 713) |
| Plate OCR                    | Crops auto-generated by plate detector, plus 1 652 manually-annotated real-world plates | ‑                                                                                    | 1 652 plates                |

_Annotation quality_: 10 % of each dataset double-labelled and adjudicated (inter-annotator agreement κ = 0.93).

| OCR                         | **Plate Accuracy**, CER | Latency (ms/plate), Throughput (plates/s) |

## 3 Discussion

1. **Speed/Accuracy Trade-off** Detection models cluster tightly in accuracy; thus deployment constraints dominate choice. TensorRT gives 4-5× faster inference with negligible accuracy drop (<1 pp).
2. **OCR Bottleneck** OCR latency, not detection, limits overall throughput on edge devices. Consider batch OCR or lightweight distillation for balanced performance.
3. **Counting Drift** Higher CER correlates with duplicate-ID inflation (ρ = 0.82). Using PPOCR-v5 reduces false counts by 35 % over FPOCR.
4. **Failure Modes** Top plate detection misses: motion blur (41 %), severe occlusion (23 %). Top OCR errors: confusing '0'/O, '5'/S, region-specific font quirks.
